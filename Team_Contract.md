## Project Focus
The delivery of a functional application that at a minimum delivers the core functionality of storing, organizing, searching for, and retrieval of literature and other media.

If time, additional quality of life tools on top of the base functionality to develop the application into a more consumer friendly and useful application. Such quality of life tools may include additional GUI features, more robust remote access tools, integration into browsers via an extension, advanced method of sorting, summarization capabilities, and more.

## Individual Expectations
The completion of tasks in a timely manner to ensure the delivery of the project.

Consistent and timely communication about changes in timeline delivery and availability to meet.

## Meeting Frequency
Check-ins on progress and task delivery on a biweekly basis.
