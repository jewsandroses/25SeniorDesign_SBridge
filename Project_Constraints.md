# Project Constraints

Due to the nature of the design intentions of this project there are some economic hurdles that must be acknowledged if this were to become a full fledged product. The intention of this software is for it to be able to be self hosted and run by its users. Other similar software’s monetization structures are based upon premium subscription models or embedded advertisements to ensure the long term profitability of their products. The self-hosted and open source nature of this project precludes these paths of monetization, but it also removes many of the long term costs -- such as server space -- which are also associated with similar products. If this product were to be turned into a product that was supported long term these obstacles would need to be investigated more thoroughly.

Another consideration that needs to be made is the legal implication of hosting various forms of media. Hosting services are generally exempt from the legal burden of taking the copyright implications of their user’s actions into account, especially when the software provider is not hosting the software directly. It is, however, possible that users could use this software to facilitate the storage, sharing, or distribution of pirated intellectual property. This should not be an immediate legal liability for the production of the software, but if the laws around copyrighted work were to change in the future, this liability should be taken into account.

A final consideration that should be noted is the general academic service that the creation of this software would provide. As noted at the beginning of this essay, the intention of this software is to be a self-host & open source version of other media repositories. These softwares are traditionally subscription services that host their resources on private servers and charge additional fees for premium features or additional space. This software would be able to provide this service for free, allowing the collection and organization of academic articles to be streamlined for free for our users.
